                        How Paradigme works

                        Paradigme organizes research into an executive synthesis, sector impacts, decision branches, and conditional scenarios. Choose Classic for sector analysis or Turchin for an exploratory historical-cycle perspective.

                        The workflow generates search queries, collects public sources, asks your configured LLM to analyze excerpts, checks basic output quality, and optionally produces PDF and text reports. Completed results remain in local history.

                        Interpreting the results

                        Check cited sources and distinguish evidence from interpretation. Quality scores check structure and citation coverage; they do not establish truth. Probabilities, PSI scores, phase positions, and timelines are LLM-generated judgments, not calibrated forecasts or a validated scientific model. The predator/prey curve is illustrative. Long-term scenarios are especially uncertain.

                        Data and connections

                        Search queries go to the selected public research services. Source excerpts and your subject/context go to your configured LLM, which may be a cloud provider. Email delivery is optional. Configuration, credentials, logs, and results stay in ignored local files. Keep the server on the loopback interface.

                        Getting useful results

                        Use a specific subject, define a place and period when relevant, and add necessary context. Avoid including private details you do not want sent to an external provider. Review evidence before acting on any generated output.
